Frequently asked questions

Do I need SPM?

No. Beginners can join.

How long is the flagship programme?

2 years: 1 year at the training centre + 1 year hotel practical at a 5-star hotel.

How many certificates can I receive?

9 per course: SKM Levels 1–3, centre professional diplomas, a diploma, an advanced diploma, and a hotel practical certificate.

Is practical training provided?

Yes — placements are not limited to Sabah; other places are possible too.

How much is the allowance?

RM1,500 per month or above.

Can I pay by instalment?

Interest-free instalment plans are available.

Can I apply for financial aid?

We can help eligible students apply.

Do you have children’s, hobby, or private classes?

Yes. Choose them on the form and we will arrange dates.

How do I register?

Fill the form. WhatsApp opens to Chef Ang with your details. Or tap WhatsApp.

Can I find a job after graduation?

We help with job placement, and partner hotels often take practical students on as permanent staff.

Do you provide accommodation for outstation students?

Yes, accommodation is available for outstation students. Ask Chef Ang for details.
